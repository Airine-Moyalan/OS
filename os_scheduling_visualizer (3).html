About this visualizer

This single-page learning tool demonstrates five disk scheduling algorithms and two CPU scheduling algorithms.

Disk scheduling

FCFS serves requests in arrival order. SSTF selects the nearest pending request. SCAN moves to the disk boundary before reversing. C-SCAN moves in one direction, jumps to the opposite boundary and continues. LOOK reverses at the last pending request instead of travelling to the physical boundary.

CPU scheduling

FCFS executes processes in arrival order without preemption. Round Robin gives each ready process a fixed time quantum and moves unfinished processes to the back of the queue.

Metrics

Turnaround time = Completion time − Arrival time. Waiting time = Turnaround time − Burst time. Average values are calculated across all processes.

Input validation

Disk requests and head positions must be within the selected cylinder range. CPU arrival times must be zero or greater, and burst times and the Round Robin quantum must be positive.
